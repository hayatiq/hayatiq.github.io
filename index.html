How to Order

1. Browse & Choose

Explore our products by category and pick what fits your routine.

2. Add to Cart

Tap the cart icon on any product — no account needed.

3. Confirm Your Order

  • Your order goes straight to our team — no accounts, no waiting on hold.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cash on Delivery really available?

Yes — every order is Cash on Delivery. You can check your item when the courier arrives and pay in cash right then, no advance payment needed.

Do I need to create an account to order?

No account needed. Add items to your cart, fill in your name, phone number, and delivery address at checkout, and place your order.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver nationwide — Inside Dhaka (৳80) and Outside Dhaka (৳130).

Is the Magnesium Oil Spray safe for sensitive skin?

We recommend a patch test on a small area first. A mild tingling on first use is normal and fades with regular use — you can dilute it with water if needed. Avoid the eyes, mouth, broken skin, and freshly shaved skin, and it's for external use only.

How do I get in touch if I have a question?

Message us on Facebook, or use the Contact page.
